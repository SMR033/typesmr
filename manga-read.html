Homunculus

Homunculus follows a man named Susumu Nakoshi, whose life takes a strange turn after he agrees to undergo an unusual medical procedure. What starts as a simple experiment soon becomes something much more disturbing, as he gains the ability to see distorted representations of people's inner selves. The manga dives deep into the human mind, exploring trauma, identity, and the darker sides of human nature. What makes Homunculus so fascinating is its unsettling atmosphere and psychological depth, constantly leaving you questioning what is real and what these strange visions actually mean. If you're into psychological manga that makes you think long after you've finished reading, Homunculus is definitely worth checking out.

Jagaaan

Jagaaan follows Shintaro Jagasaki, a police officer whose life takes a bizarre turn when he gets caught up in a strange parasite outbreak that causes people to transform into horrifying creatures. What starts as an ordinary life quickly spirals into chaos, filled with brutal fights, twisted desires, and unpredictable developments. What makes Jagaaan stand out to me is its wild artwork and dark atmosphere, pushing the boundaries of what you'd expect from a typical manga. If you're into gritty stories with over-the-top action, disturbing transformations, and a protagonist caught between his own desires and the chaos around him, Jagaaan is definitely worth checking out.

Manhole

Manhole is a three-volume bio-horror manga that follows two detectives investigating a deadly, engineered parasite spreading from the city's sewers. What begins as a disturbing case quickly turns into a race against time to stop a potentially catastrophic, man-made epidemic. What makes Manhole stand out is its unsettling atmosphere, realistic investigation, and the horrifying idea that something so small could threaten so many lives. If you're into mystery, biological horror, and suspenseful stories that keep you wondering what will happen next, Manhole is definitely worth checking out.
