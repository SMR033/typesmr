Yo, This is SMR READS!

# Yo, this is SMR READS! Welcome to SMR READS, a place where I share the manga I enjoy, the stories that stick with me, and the series I think are worth talking about. I'm Samir, and I made this site to share my love for manga through reviews, recommendations, and deep dives into the things that make each series unique. One thing I especially love about manga is the artwork and the covers. Even after finishing a series online, I'll sometimes pick up a physical volume simply because I love its cover. From the artwork and composition to the small details and hidden Easter eggs, there's often more to a cover than you might expect. Here, I'll be taking a closer look at those details while also sharing my thoughts on the stories, characters, art, and everything else that makes a manga stand out. SMR READS is still a work in progress, so expect new reviews, new manga, and a few hidden surprises along the way. Take a look around, find something interesting, and maybe discover your next read. Thanks for stopping by. Peace.
