Yo This is SMR READS!

Yo, this is SMR READS! I'm Samir, and I made this site for a school project. It's basically my little corner of the internet for all manga the manga i read, want to read, and love the most. You'll find what I've been reading lately, what's still on my list, and my favourites.

I'm kind of obsessed with covers. When I finish a series online I'll buy a volume, but only if the cover is actually good looking and interesting enough. Sometimes there are small details or Easter eggs hidden in them, so I like to look at them properly. The site isn't finished yet, so more stuff is coming. Just look around, and thanks for stopping by.
