Jojo's Bizzare Adventures

JoJo's Bizarre Adventure is one hell of a ride. Imagine a series where every part gets more insane than the last. Not only that, but the characters are incredibly well-developed, and the artwork is absolutely stunning. It's truly one of a kind. You can see how much Hirohiko Araki has grown as an artist throughout the series, constantly experimenting with his style and refining his artwork into the unique look it has today. From the creative character designs to the bizarre abilities and over-the-top battles, JoJo's always finds a way to surprise you. There's genuinely nothing else quite like it.

Black Clover

Black Clover is my all-time favourite manga. The story is engaging, the characters are incredibly well-developed, and it has a darker side to it, or should I say, a devilish side. But what makes this series so special to me is Asta. He has always been an idol of mine because no matter how many people doubted him or stood against him, he never gave up on his dreams or the people he cared about. Even when the odds were stacked against him, he kept pushing forward and refused to let others define his worth. His determination and loyalty are what make him such an inspiring character to me. Anyways, Black Clover is a must-read for any shonen lover who grew up with Naruto or other underdog stories. If you love watching someone prove everyone wrong through sheer determination, this manga is definitely for you.

Gachiakuta

Think wastelands, fashion, and a young boy with an unshakable soul. That's Rudo, the protagonist of Gachiakuta. Follow him on a journey driven by revenge after the death of his caretaker, as he navigates a brutal world filled with danger, strange abilities, and mysterious creatures. What makes Gachiakuta stand out to me is its unique art style, bold character designs, and gritty atmosphere. It has a visual identity that feels completely its own, blending streetwear-inspired fashion with chaotic, expressive artwork. If you're looking for a shonen manga that feels fresh and isn't afraid to stand out, Gachiakuta is definitely worth checking out.
