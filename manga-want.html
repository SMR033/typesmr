Dai dark

Dai Dark is a bizarre sci-fi manga that throws you into a universe filled with darkness, chaos, and some seriously weird characters. The story follows Zaha Sanko, a teenager whose bones are rumoured to grant whoever possesses them any wish. Because of this, he's constantly being hunted by people who want to claim that power. What makes Dai Dark stand out to me is its unique artwork, strange sense of humour, and the way it blends horror with ridiculous moments. The character designs are especially memorable, giving the whole series a gritty, almost otherworldly feel. If you're into unconventional manga that don't take themselves too seriously while still delivering an intriguing story, Dai Dark is definitely worth checking out.
