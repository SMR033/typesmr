Hello there!

Yo, I'm Samir! I'm a big fan of reading, hiking, and fishing, and I love getting into new worlds through JRPGs, anime, and manga. Whether it's discovering a new story, exploring the outdoors, or getting lost in a game for hours, I'm always looking for something interesting to dive into. I'm especially passionate about manga, from the stories and characters to the incredible artwork and hidden details on volume covers. I made this website to share the series I love, the ones I'm currently reading, and the ones I think deserve more attention. This website is still a work in progress, so expect things to change as I continue building it. I'll also be adding little Easter eggs and hidden buttons for you to discover along the way. Feel free to look around, explore, and maybe find your next favourite manga. Thanks for stopping by. Peace!
